Quick answer

A birthday invitation should say whose birthday it is, the date with a start and end time, the full address, how and by when to RSVP, and anything guests need to bring. Send kids’ party invitations 2–3 weeks ahead and milestone or surprise party invitations about 6 weeks ahead.

What should the invitation include?

  • Whose birthday it is (age optional for adults)
  • Date, start time and end time
  • Full address and parking notes
  • RSVP name, method and reply-by date
  • What to bring or wear

Frequently asked questions

When should birthday invitations go out?

Two to three weeks before a kids’ party, three to four weeks for an adult party, and around six weeks for milestone or surprise parties where guests may travel.

Should siblings be invited to a kids’ party?

Say it clearly on the invitation: “Siblings welcome” or address the invitation to the child only. Unclear invitations are the main cause of surprise extra guests.

How do you say “no gifts” politely?

“Your presence is the only present needed” is the most widely used wording. Put it at the bottom of the card, not the top.
