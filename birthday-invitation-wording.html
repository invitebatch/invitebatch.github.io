Birthday Invitation Wording

Birthday invitations can be as playful as you like, as long as the practical details are clear. Here is what to include, then wording for every age.

What should a birthday invitation include?

  • Whose birthday it is, and which one (ages are optional for adults!)
  • Date, start time and end time
  • Address, plus parking or entry notes
  • RSVP name, method and reply-by date
  • Anything guests need: swimsuit, costume, “no gifts please”

Kids’ birthday party wording

Come party with Leo! He’s turning 5!
Saturday, May 8, 2–4 pm
Jump Zone, 22 Park Road
RSVP to Sam by May 1
Calling all superheroes! Join Mia for her 7th birthday mission.
Roar into Max’s dino-mite 4th birthday party!
Grab your swimsuit — it’s a pool party for Ava’s 9th!
Ages 6 and up, drop-off party. Pick-up at 4:30 pm.

First birthday wording

Our little one is turning ONE! Join us to celebrate Noah’s first birthday.
One sweet year. Please join us for cake and smiles as Lily turns one.
Wild one! Celebrate Theo’s first trip around the sun.
Oh, what fun — Ella is turning one!

Milestone birthday wording (30th, 40th, 50th and beyond)

Cheers to 30 years! Join us for drinks and dancing to celebrate Jordan.
Forty and fabulous. Please join us for dinner honoring Rachel’s 40th.
Fifty years young! Join us in celebrating Michael’s milestone birthday.
Join us for a celebration of 80 wonderful years — honoring Grandma Rose.
Aged to perfection: a wine evening for Paul’s 60th.

Surprise party wording

The most important line on a surprise invitation is the secret instruction — and the arrival time, which should be before the guest of honour arrives.

Shh… it’s a surprise! Join us for Dana’s 40th. Please arrive by 6:30 pm sharp — Dana arrives at 7.
Surprise! We’re throwing Chris a party and he has no idea. Mum’s the word.

No gifts, please

Your presence is the only present needed.
No gifts, please — but bring your best birthday story.
In place of gifts, donations to the local animal shelter are warmly welcomed.

When should birthday invitations go out?

Two to three weeks before a kids’ party, three to four weeks for an adult party, and six weeks for milestone or surprise parties where people travel.
