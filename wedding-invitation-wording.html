Wedding Invitation Wording

Wedding invitations follow a fixed order, and once you know it, the wording almost writes itself. Below: the order, then examples for every hosting situation.

What order does wedding invitation wording follow?

  1. Host line — who is inviting.
  2. Request line — “request the honour of your presence” (religious venue) or “request the pleasure of your company” (other venues).
  3. Couple’s names.
  4. Date and time, spelled out on formal cards.
  5. Venue and city.
  6. Reception line, such as “Dinner and dancing to follow”.

Parents of the bride hosting

Mr. and Mrs. David Allen
request the honour of your presence
at the marriage of their daughter
Claire Elizabeth
to
Thomas James Reed
Saturday, the twelfth of June
two thousand twenty-seven
at four o’clock in the afternoon
St. Mark’s Church, Charleston, South Carolina

Both families hosting

Mr. and Mrs. David Allen
and
Mr. and Mrs. Robert Reed
request the pleasure of your company
at the marriage of their children
Claire Elizabeth and Thomas James
Together with their families
Claire Allen and Thomas Reed
invite you to celebrate their marriage

The couple hosting

Claire Allen and Thomas Reed
request the pleasure of your company
at their marriage
We’re getting married!
Claire and Tom
invite you to celebrate with them

Blended and divorced families

Each divorced parent gets their own line, with no “and” joining them as a couple. Stepparents can be included alongside the parent they are married to.

Ms. Linda Allen
Mr. and Mrs. David Allen
request the honour of your presence
at the marriage of their daughter…
Claire Elizabeth Allen,
daughter of Linda Allen and the late David Allen,
and Thomas James Reed…

Reception-only invitations

Please join us for a reception
to celebrate the marriage of
Claire Allen and Thomas Reed
Saturday, June 12, 2027, at seven o’clock in the evening
Claire and Tom were married in a private ceremony.
Please join them for dinner, drinks and dancing.

Destination weddings

Claire Allen and Thomas Reed
invite you to join them in Lisbon, Portugal
for their wedding weekend
June 10–13, 2027
Travel details at claireandtom.com

What should not go on a wedding invitation?

  • Registry information — put it on the wedding website.
  • “Adults only” — show it with names on the envelope and seat counts on the RSVP card.
  • Abbreviations on formal cards (“St.”, “Sat.”).
