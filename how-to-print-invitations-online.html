Quick answer

To print an invitation online, export your design as a PDF (in Canva: Share → Download → PDF Print), upload it to an online printer, choose the size, quantity, card and envelopes, check the preview, then pay. On InviteBatch this takes about 5 minutes with no account. Printing takes 1–3 working days, and you can order any number from 1 to 300 cards.

Step 1: export the right file

Most print problems start here. The printer can only print what is in your file, so spend two minutes on this.

From Canva

Choose Share → Download → PDF Print. Tick “Crop marks and bleed” if your colour or images run to the edge of the card. Don’t use PDF Standard, which is made for screens.

From Microsoft Word

Choose File → Save As → PDF. Set the page to your card size before you save, or the design will arrive with wide white margins.

From an Etsy template

Most Etsy invitations open in Canva or Templett. Finish your edits there and download the print PDF. If the seller sent a sheet with several cards on one page, go back and download a single card instead.

Only have a PNG or JPG?

That works if it is big enough. Aim for 300 pixels per inch:

  • 6×4″ card: 1800 × 1200 pixels
  • 7×5″ card: 2100 × 1500 pixels
  • 6×6″ square: 1800 × 1800 pixels

A screenshot or an image saved from WhatsApp is usually too small and prints soft.

Bleed and safe area, in plain words

A printer cuts cards from bigger sheets, and the cut can move very slightly. Two simple rules stop that from showing:

  • Bleed: if colour runs to the edge, let it run past the edge, so no white sliver appears after cutting.
  • Safe area: keep names, dates and addresses well inside the edge, so nothing gets trimmed.

How to order on InviteBatch, step by step

  1. Open the tool. Go to print invitations online. You don’t need an account.
  2. Upload your file. A two-page PDF prints as front and back. With more pages, you tap the ones you want.
  3. Check the preview. Look at the trim and safe-area lines on both sides, and read the warnings under the card.
  4. Choose the size and quantity. Order 5–10 more cards than you need, for keepsakes and late guests.
  5. Choose sides, card and envelopes. Printing both sides costs the same as one.
  6. Check out. Add your email and address, then pick a delivery option. Each one shows its price and arrival date.
  7. Approve and pay. Tick that your design is correct, then pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal.

Check these 7 things before you approve

Printed cards can’t be corrected, so read the preview slowly, out loud if you can.

  • Every name is spelled correctly
  • The weekday matches the date
  • The time says morning, afternoon or evening
  • The venue name and address are complete
  • The reply-by date is before your caterer’s deadline
  • Any QR code or link works when you test it on your phone
  • No template text is left, on the front or the back

Frequently asked questions

What is the best file format for printing invitations?

A PDF. Text stays sharp at any size, while a PNG or JPG depends on its pixel size. If you only have an image, aim for 300 pixels per inch: 2100 × 1500 pixels for a 7×5″ card.

How do I print a Canva invitation?

In Canva choose Share → Download → PDF Print, and tick “Crop marks and bleed” if your design has colour or images running to the edge. Upload that PDF to a print service such as InviteBatch. Page 1 becomes the front and page 2 the back.

Can I print just one invitation?

Yes. InviteBatch prints from 1 card. A single 7×5″ matte card with an envelope costs £9.94 with standard UK post, so it is also a cheap way to see the real card before you order the full set.

How much does it cost to print 50 invitations?

On InviteBatch, 50 7×5″ matte cards with envelopes cost £109.50, or £118.05 with tracked UK courier. 50 7×5″ gloss cards with envelopes are £1.79 each. Prices checked 30 October 2026.

How long does printing take?

Cards are printed in 1–3 working days, then delivery depends on the option you pick. Every option shows its price and expected arrival date before you pay.

Do you deliver outside the UK?

Yes: the United States, Canada and 13 EU countries. Prices are worked out for your country at checkout, and small orders abroad cost noticeably more because of delivery. Import charges may apply in the US and Canada.

Can I get my money back if I spot a typo after ordering?

No. Cards are made to order and printed exactly as you approve them, so there are no change-of-mind returns. If a card arrives faulty or damaged, send a photo within 14 days and it is reprinted free or refunded.
