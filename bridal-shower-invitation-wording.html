Bridal Shower Invitation Wording

A bridal shower invitation usually comes from the host, not the bride, and it can mention gifts — two things that make it different from the wedding invitation itself.

What should a bridal shower invitation include?

  1. The bride’s name (and partner’s, for a couples shower)
  2. Date, start and end time
  3. Location and address
  4. Host name and RSVP details
  5. Registry or theme notes (on the card or an insert)

Classic wording

Please join us for a bridal shower honoring Claire Allen
Saturday, April 17, 2027, at 1 pm
Hosted by Jade Kim
RSVP to Jade by April 7
With love and laughter, please join us in showering the bride-to-be, Claire Allen.
Before she says “I do”, let’s shower Claire with love.

Brunch and tea party wording

Bubbles and brunch for the bride-to-be!
Love is brewing — join us for a bridal tea honoring Claire.
Mimosas, macarons and the future Mrs. Reed.

Themed shower wording

Around-the-clock shower: each guest will receive an hour of the day — please bring a gift to match.
Stock the bar! Help Claire and Tom fill their home bar.
Recipe shower: please bring your favourite recipe along with a kitchen gift.
She found her main squeeze — a lemon-themed shower for Claire.

Couples shower wording

Join us for a couples shower honoring Claire and Tom.
Two hearts, one home: a co-ed shower for the happy couple.

Who hosts a bridal shower, and who is invited?

Traditionally a close friend or the maid of honour hosts; today family hosting is common too. Only invite people who are also invited to the wedding — the one exception is a workplace shower thrown by colleagues.

When should bridal shower invitations go out?

Four to six weeks before the shower. Showers usually happen two to three months before the wedding.
