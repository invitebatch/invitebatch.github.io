Bridal shower invitation wording

A bridal shower invitation comes from the host, not the bride, and it can mention gifts — two things that make it different from the wedding invitation.

Quick answer

A bridal shower invitation names the bride (and partner for a couples shower), the date with start and end time, the location, the host and RSVP details, and optionally the registry or theme. Send it four to six weeks before the shower, and only invite people who are invited to the wedding.

What should the invitation include?

  • The bride’s name (and partner’s, for a couples shower)
  • Date, start and end time
  • Location and address
  • Host name and RSVP details
  • Registry or theme notes (card or insert)

Frequently asked questions

Who hosts a bridal shower?

Traditionally a close friend or the maid of honour. Today family hosting is common too, and co-hosting with a few friends is the norm.

Who should be invited to the bridal shower?

Only people who are also invited to the wedding. The one exception is a workplace shower thrown by colleagues.

When should bridal shower invitations be sent?

Four to six weeks before the shower. Showers usually take place two to three months before the wedding.

Can registry information go on a shower invitation?

Yes. Unlike the wedding invitation, a shower invitation can include registry details, ideally on a small insert.
