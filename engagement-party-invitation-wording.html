Engagement party invitation wording

Engagement parties are the most relaxed event of the wedding calendar, and the wording can be too. The one rule that matters: invite only people you plan to invite to the wedding.

Quick answer

An engagement party invitation names the couple, the host, the date, time and place, and how to RSVP. Send it three to four weeks before the party, leave gift requests off, and only invite guests who will also be invited to the wedding.

What should the invitation include?

  • The couple’s names
  • The host (parents, friends or the couple)
  • Date, time and place
  • RSVP details
  • Dress code, if it is not obvious

Formal engagement party wording

Usually for a parent-hosted dinner or reception.

Casual wording

Drinks, dinners and backyard parties.

Surprise engagement parties

Usually planned around a proposal — keep the secret line prominent.

Frequently asked questions

Do you bring a gift to an engagement party?

Engagement gifts are optional. Many couples have not set up a registry yet, so a card or a small gift is plenty.

Can you write “no gifts” on an engagement party invitation?

Yes, if you want to: “No gifts, please — your company is the perfect gift.” Otherwise leave gifts off entirely.

When should engagement party invitations go out?

Three to four weeks before the party, which usually happens within two to three months of the proposal.
